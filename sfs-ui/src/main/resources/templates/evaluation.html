Evaluation

Fidelity is the measured difference between an original document and its reconstruction. Semantic, structural and factual quality are reported separately, because a reconstruction can read well and still lose a date or a quantity.

When fidelity cannot be measured

Measuring fidelity requires the original document. Once a file's raw bytes have been deleted there is nothing left to compare against, so no score is produced and none is estimated. Those cases appear below as Not measurable.
